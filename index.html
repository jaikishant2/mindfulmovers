OUR STORY

About Mindful Movers

♟ Coach P.S.Jaikishan — Founder & Head Coach

Mindful Movers Chess Club is a premier chess coaching academy founded in Chennai, India by Coach P.S.Jaikishan. With a passion for chess and a gift for teaching, Coach Jaikishan has built an academy that turns raw beginners into competitive champions.

Offering both in-person classes in Chennai and online coaching for students worldwide, our reach extends to India, the USA, UAE, UK, Australia and beyond. Distance is no barrier to learning great chess.

Our curriculum is structured, progressive, and tailored — covering openings, tactics, endgames, and tournament psychology. Every student gets the personal attention they deserve.

WHY CHESS?

More Than a Game.
A Life Skill.

Science-backed benefits that go far beyond the 64 squares — endorsed by educators worldwide.

🧩

Sharpens Problem-Solving

Chess trains the brain to analyze complex situations, think ahead, and devise creative solutions — directly transferable to academics and real life challenges.

🧠

Boosts Memory & Focus

Memorizing openings, recognizing patterns, and sustained concentration across long games dramatically strengthens both working and long-term memory.

♟

Builds Strategic Thinking

Planning multiple moves ahead teaches children to evaluate trade-offs, think systematically, and make confident decisions under pressure.

🌟

Develops Resilience

Winning graciously, losing with dignity — chess builds the mental toughness and sportsmanship that carries into every area of a child's life.
